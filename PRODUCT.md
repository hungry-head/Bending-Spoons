# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent and group travelers planning, coordinating, and taking multi-day trips. They use TripUp before travel to generate plans and organize logistics, and during travel to follow schedules, discover places, manage shared expenses, and settle debts.

## Product Purpose

TripUp is an AI travel companion that turns destination, dates, pace, budget, and group preferences into an editable day-by-day itinerary. It keeps planning, maps, place discovery, schedules, group collaboration, expenses, debt settlement, and trip memories together so travelers spend less time coordinating and more time exploring.

## Brand Personality

Capable, optimistic, and practical. The interface should feel like a dependable mobile travel utility: bright, compact, clear, and friendly without becoming playful at the expense of dense planning and financial information.

## Anti-references

Avoid cinematic travel-editorial layouts, oversized destination photography, warm lifestyle branding, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized headings, and generic AI chat-first interfaces. Do not revive the earlier warm coral and paper-like concept. The current Atlix TripUp Android application is the visual and interaction reference.

## Design Principles

1. Put today’s trip state and the next useful action first.
2. Keep complex itinerary and financial data compact, scannable, and editable.
3. Use AI as an assistive planning layer, not as the entire interface.
4. Preserve familiar mobile navigation and standard control behavior.
5. Make group state, budget health, and schedule changes immediately legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support screen readers, voice control, larger text, sufficient contrast, non-color state differentiation, dark-interface readiness, and reduced motion. Maintain at least 44pt touch targets and accessible labels for icon-only controls. Maps require text alternatives and visible OpenStreetMap attribution.
